Frequently Asked Questions

Will this jeopardise my primary company domain?

No. We conduct 100% of outbound activity through isolated secondary lookalike domains. Your primary domain is never used and remains completely safe.

How much time is required from my team?

Less than 1 hour per week. We handle domain setup, list sourcing, copywriting, and technical warm-ups. Your only job is replying to warm leads and hosting the sales calls.

Why is there a 2-month minimum commitment?

Outbound engines require proper ramping to protect your domain. During Weeks 1–2 of Month 1, we handle technical setup, contact verification, and a mandatory 14-day domain warmup. Live campaigns launch in Weeks 3–4 at a controlled pace. Month 2 is where we scale to full sending volume and compound your positive reply momentum into booked calls.

How do you handle compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL)?

We strictly follow B2B outreach regulations in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. All outreach uses verified business contacts, plain-text email formatting, and simple opt-out phrasing (e.g., "reply pass to update list"), ensuring full compliance while protecting inbox deliverability.

What makes Taskify different from traditional lead gen agencies?

Many agencies send aggressive sales pitches from a single inbox, burning domain reputations and landing in spam. We build multi-domain email networks that protect your primary brand, combined with low-pressure messaging designed specifically to book high-ticket corporate search retainers.

Do I need to provide my own lead lists?

No. We handle end-to-end list building and multi-pass email verification based on your ideal client criteria (industry, headcount, geography, and executive titles). You never have to supply data or source leads yourself.
